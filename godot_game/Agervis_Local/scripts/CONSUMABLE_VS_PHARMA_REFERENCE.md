## General Decision Matrix

### Choose Pharmaceuticals When:
- Need **instant full-power** effects
- Willing to accept **addiction risk**
- **Emergency/combat** situation
- Want **maximum effect strength**
- Have access to **prescriptions**

### Choose Consumables When:
- Want **zero addiction risk**
- Can tolerate **gradual/ramping** effects
- **Out of combat** or preparing ahead
- Need **sustained duration** over power
- Want **cheaper, accessible** options
- Can manage **food block cooldown**

### Choose Street Substances When:
- Need **absolute maximum power** (Crystal, China)
- Willing to accept **extreme addiction**
- **Life-or-death** emergency (China instant heal)
- Accept risk of **lethal overdose**

## Stacking Strategies

### Safe Combinations (No dangerous interactions):
- **Food + Food** (different categories) - Food block is only limit
- **Food + Pharma** (non-conflicting) - Ex: Energy Drink + Vassalium
- **Multiple foods over time** - Wait for food block to clear

### Dangerous Combinations (Avoid):
- **Opioid + CNS depressant** - Extreme overdose risk
- **Multiple pharmas same category** - Overdose triggers
- **Street + Pharma same effect** - Unpredictable stacking

### Optimal Pre-Combat Loadout Examples:

**Aggressive DPS:**
- Energy Drink (accuracy/attack speed) + Protein Shake (guard ramp) + Trail Mix (energy sustain)
- Wait for ramps, engage at full power

**Defensive Tank:**
- Recovery Meal (guard/muscle ramp) + Fermented Drink (vitality floor) + Spicy Noodles (CC reduction)
- Maximum survivability, no addiction

**Ranged Specialist:**
- Mushroom Soup (ballistic evasion) + Strong Coffee (hands/energy) + Calming Tea (CC resist)
- Stay mobile, pick shots, sustain composure

**Budget Emergency Kit:**
- Bone Broth (big heal over time) + High-Calorie Bar (quick energy) + Capsaicin Dish (CC protection)
- Cheap, effective, covers basics
